2024 KU bioconference 
======================
This project begins with the creation of computational models of plant immune receptors using protein structural modeling software. From the generated structural models, we hope to visualize the binding interface between the receptor and the ligand and then introduce mutations at the binding interface to evaluate the binding using molecular docking software.
